# Coaching, correction and follow-up

For each useful feedback item connect the episode and observable contribution to
its consequence/uncertainty, then a practical move. Prefer one primary target to
a long list of aspirations. Keep a supported strength to maintain when available;
do not invent one to balance a criticism.

A practice plan records:

- **Why:** episode/claim IDs and the gap or strength to work on.
- **Opportunity:** next real task or a clearly proposed small exercise.
- **Human action:** a decision, prediction, comparison or interpretation under the
  person's control; clarify what the LLM may assist with.
- **Evidence:** artifact/observation to retain without oversharing.
- **Check:** an observable success criterion and what would count against it.
- **Revisit:** after the declared task/checkpoint, without implying an automatic
  reminder, scheduled service or completed exercise.

Example: if a requirement had only an assistant's success statement, the person
can select one consequential requirement, name a failure that would contradict it,
choose a check and interpret its actual receipt. The assistant may help prepare
the check. Success is a retained requirement/check/result comparison and a decision
that responds to a failure; an unsupported “it worked” is counterevidence.
Do not infer implementation expertise from selecting that check.

## Revisit an existing plan

Start with the earlier target, baseline and evidence. Compare only where there is
a comparable opportunity: task difficulty, assistance, tools, constraints and
observation coverage may differ. State those differences and whether they prevent
a conclusion. A later observed human action can support a bounded change in that
episode; it does not prove general transfer, sector novelty or improvement caused
by coaching. Stated intention is pending, not completed behavior.

Use **observed in this opportunity**, **not yet observed**, or **comparison
inconclusive**, with sources. These are states of evidence, not ability grades.
If no later material exists, retain the plan as untested and specify what to collect.
Do not fabricate a baseline or follow-up result to finish the report.

## Correct an earlier interpretation

Retain stable IDs, identify the new source, say which claim it corrects and why,
and show remaining uncertainty. Revisit the practice advice and any dependent
shareable draft. A private correction must not leave an inflated CV claim intact.
Keep a concise correction trail; do not overwrite uncertainty with the most recent
assertion merely because it is recent.

Feedback is a hypothesis the person can challenge. Ask a narrow question when a
missing offline decision would materially alter the conclusion; otherwise finish
the supported work. The coach provides a formative reading, not technical audit,
psychological diagnosis, assessment certification or an employment decision.
