# Personal project coaching — private working report

Adapt this outline; omit irrelevant sections. It is not a mandatory quota or a
psychometric form. Do not fill gaps with invented observations.

## Scope and evidence

Person/role as recorded; selected project/materials and goal; selection gaps;
what was analyzed and what was not verified. Stable source IDs/locators.

## Episodes and contributions

For each material episode: stable episode/claim IDs; exact source excerpt/locator;
goal and known constraints; information available then; visible proposer,
selector/modifier, checker/interpreter; later outcome and its support; gaps,
contradictions and alternative readings. Separate event from interpretation.

## What the episodes suggest

Supported strengths to retain and difficulties to address, each with episode IDs,
observable human/model/team contributions, consequence/limit and next move.
Relevant lenses: systems thinking, critical thinking, idea integration, product
orientation, delegation, verification/responsibility, learning/adaptation.
Unobserved areas remain unobserved; no overall score or professional rank.

## One practice and follow-up

Reason/episode; next opportunity; human action and LLM assistance; evidence to
collect; success criterion and counterexample; revisit trigger. State what remains
a proposal and what has actually been observed. Compare with an earlier baseline
only where conditions/evidence are sufficiently comparable.

## Corrections and sharing

Unresolved material questions and affected claim IDs; concise correction trail if
updating a prior report. Only if a CV/portfolio draft is requested: private
claim/evidence/permission map and separate shareable output or reason for withholding.
Never append this private report or its source excerpts automatically to that draft.
